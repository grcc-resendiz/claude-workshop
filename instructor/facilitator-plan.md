## Before the room fills

- Open the guide (github.com/grcc-resendiz/claude-workshop) on the projector machine and confirm every prompt's copy button works.
- Run Steps 3, 4, 6, and 7 once on the Claude Max account you will present from, using a fictional business, in a new empty folder outside your own workspace (for example Documents/Claude Sample). Skip Steps 1 and 2 so your Settings stay as they are, and delete the folder afterward if you like. Confirm Claude researches prices, stops at the warranty, and applies the 10 percent discount in a new chat. Leave that workspace open as the example to show.
- Open your own workspace in the Claude desktop app for the 5:45 demo.
- Test Wi-Fi with a second device. Keep a hotspot ready.
- Parking vouchers and Claude Max codes at check in.

## Where people get stuck

- **Steps 1 and 2:** finding the Instructions box. Initials in the lower left, Settings, Your Claude, Instructions. Show it on screen, slowly, twice.
- **Step 3:** they paste the prompt into a chat without clicking Work in a folder first. Check screens as you roam.
- **Step 4:** a one word answer about the business ("consulting") gives Step 6 too little to research. Coach a real sentence: what they sell and to whom.
- **Step 6:** the guardrails make Claude show a plan and wait, and the web research takes a minute or two. Say both out loud before they paste. If web search is off, they reply "Use typical prices you know and label them estimated."
- **Step 7:** they keep going in the same chat for Prompt 4. A new chat with the folder selected is what proves the folder did the work. If the discount is missing, have them say "Read the Quote Creator HQ MEMORY.md and redo the quote."
- **Step 10:** work accounts blocked by IT, and sign in windows hiding behind the app. Highest failure rate of the night. Pair people up.

## Pairing and pace

Jeremy roams during every hands on block. Cap one on one help at two minutes, then pair the person with a neighbor who is done. Anyone who finishes a part early starts the next step in the guide.

## What to collect

At 7:00, ask three people what kind of business their HQ quoted for and what price list Claude found. Those are the examples for the next workshop.
