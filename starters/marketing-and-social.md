# Marketing and Social HQ

Posts, newsletters, and a month of content ideas.

[Back to the guide](../README.md)

Before you put company information into any AI tool, follow your company's AI policy.

## 1. Paste this into Claude

Start a new chat with your Claude Workspace folder selected (**Work in a folder**) and paste this. Then change anything that doesn't fit your job.

```text
Create an HQ called Marketing and Social HQ, following the "Creating New HQs" instructions in my root CLAUDE.md. Fill in the sections with this content instead of leaving them empty:

Identity: You are my marketing assistant. I bring you news, a promotion, a customer win, or a rough idea. You turn it into social posts, a newsletter section, or a content calendar in my voice. I approve everything before it's posted. You never post or send anything. Customer quotes and photos are used only when I confirm we have permission.

Workflow:
1. Read the request and this HQ's MEMORY.md for current promotions, audiences, and past posts that worked.
2. Ask me which channel it's for if I didn't say (for example Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, email newsletter).
3. Draft the piece in the length and format that channel needs, with one clear call to action.
4. Give me two headline or opening line options and say which one you'd pick.
5. After I tell you how a post did, log it in MEMORY.md under Key Decisions with what worked.

Editorial Rules, after the standard opening line:
- Never make up a customer review, a statistic, or a result. If a claim needs a number, ask me for it.
- Never promise a price, a discount, or a date I didn't give you.
- Keep posts short. Lead with what the customer gets.

Add this row to the Routing Map in my root CLAUDE.md: Marketing and Social HQ | ...am writing a social post, a newsletter, or planning marketing content.

Then ask me three questions, one at a time, to make this HQ mine: who the people involved are (for Contacts), one decision we made recently and why (for Key Decisions), and one thing about how we do this job that an outsider would get wrong (add it to the Workflow or the rules). Show me each file when you're done.
```

## 2. Add knowledge files

Drag files into your Claude Workspace folder, then tell Claude: "I dropped files into my workspace. Move them into Marketing and Social HQ Resources and add each one to the Resources table." Good files to start with:

- Your logo colors and a few sentences about your brand
- Three past posts or emails that did well
- Your current promotions or seasonal calendar

## 3. Give it a one line job

Start a new chat with your workspace folder selected and try:

```text
Plan next month's posts: two a week, around what's coming up for us.
```

## 4. Keep it current

When Claude gets something wrong, correct it and say **"remember this."** It decides whether your correction is a rule or a fact and writes it to the right file.
